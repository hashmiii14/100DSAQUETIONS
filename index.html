About FAANG DSA Forge

FAANG DSA Forge is a clean, modern, developer-focused problem sheet designed to take software engineers from programming fundamentals to advanced FAANG interview problem solving.

The 1000 Curriculum

The curriculum consists of exactly 1000 real and verified LeetCode problems categorized into 200 Easy, 500 Medium, and 300 Hard questions covering key patterns like Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Monotonic Stack, Binary Search, Trees, Graphs, and Dynamic Programming.

How Students Use It

Follow the FAANG DSA Forge Learning Path, pick a topic, start solving, and track your progress locally in your browser. All bookmarks, notes, and progress persistence happen automatically via local storage (`localStorage`).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

At FAANG DSA Forge, we value your privacy. This policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our web platform.

2. Information We Collect & Local Storage

We do not require user account registration. All problem-solving progress, solved problem IDs, personal notes, bookmarks, and theme preferences are saved locally in your Web Browser using localStorage (`dsaproblems_done_v3`, `dsaproblems_bookmarked_v3`, `dsaproblems_notes_v3`, `dsaproblems_theme_v3`). No personal data or progress statistics are transmitted to our servers.

3. External Problem Links

External practice links safely open verified problem statements directly on LeetCode in a new browser tab.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics trackers to profile user identity. Standard AdSense script configurations comply with Google publisher guidelines.
